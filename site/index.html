Setup

Ready in three steps.

Android

  1. Tap Android · APK and accept the download.
  2. Open creator-1.79.apk from your downloads.
  3. Allow installing unknown apps if Android asks, then install.

Windows PC

  1. On a PC, click Windows PC and save creator-1.79-pc.zip.
  2. Right-click the file, choose Extract All, then double-click Creator.exe.
  3. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info then Run anyway.
  4. Keyboard: W A S D or the arrow keys to move, Space to jump, the mouse for the buttons.

Mac

  1. Click Mac and open creator-1.79-mac.zip: you get Creator.app.
  2. Drag Creator.app into Applications.
  3. The first time, macOS blocks it: open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

iPhone

  1. On iPhone, tap iPhone · play in Safari: the game starts right away, nothing to install.
  2. To keep it like an app: Safari's Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Turn the phone sideways. The first load takes a little while (8 MB).
